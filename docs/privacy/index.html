Privacy Policy for Nextel

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Nextel ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Nextel mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses and how it's handled.

Information Stored on Your Device

When you set up Nextel, the App asks for:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

This information, along with your progress and activity in the App, is stored only on your own device. Nextel does not have a server or backend, and this information is never transmitted off your device, sent to us, or shared with any third party.

How This Information Is Used

  • To personalize your experience within the App (e.g. displaying your name)
  • To let you resume your progress within the App on the same device

Data Sharing

We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information, because it never leaves your device. Nextel has no servers and no third-party integrations that access this data.

Data Storage & Security

Since your information stays on your device, its security depends on your device's own security (e.g. screen lock, OS updates). We recommend keeping your device secured.

Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete all information stored by Nextel at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it from your device. See our account deletion page for details.

Children's Privacy

Nextel is not directed at children under 13, and the App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if Nextel introduces account syncing or server-based features in the future. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
