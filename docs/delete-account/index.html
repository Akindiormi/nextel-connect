What Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, we delete:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for a limited period after your request — for example, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security logs. Any such data is automatically deleted within 90 days of your deletion request.

Partial Data Deletion

At this time, we do not offer a way to delete specific pieces of data without deleting your full account. Requesting deletion removes your entire account and associated data as described above.
