Delete Your Nextel Account

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Nextel stores your account information (name, email address, and phone number) only on your own device. We do not have a server that stores or holds your data, so there is nothing on our end to delete.

How to Delete Your Data

You can remove all your Nextel data at any time by either:

  • Clearing app data — on Android, go to Settings → Apps → Nextel → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the app — this permanently removes all locally stored information

Both actions immediately and permanently delete your name, email, phone number, and any progress or activity saved within the app.
